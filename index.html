Why I moved into AI

From predicting football matches to finding the patterns behind intelligence.

  1. 01 · The first spark

    A decision tree and a football match

    In an undergraduate AI class, we built a decision tree to answer one question: should a football match go ahead, given the weather? Watching patterns in data turn into a decision fascinated me. At the time, I didn't follow it further.

  2. 02 · The turning point

    The same bug, again and again

    As a Software Engineer at Aequitas Infotech, I noticed one bug showing up for several users. I traced the pattern to its root cause, fixed it, and told my team. My senior manager was impressed that I saw the pattern, and encouraged me to pursue a Master's degree.

  3. 03 · The leap

    A new country, a new field

    That conversation became a turning point. I moved to the United States to study for a Master's in Computer Science at Loyola Marymount University in Los Angeles.

  4. 04 · The present

    Following the patterns

    Now I spend my time on machine learning, deep learning and generative AI: building projects, and serving as the Teaching Assistant for graduate Machine Learning at LMU.
